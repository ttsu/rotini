Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

Rotini ("we", "us", or "our") provides shared rota scheduling for groups. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Rotini mobile app, why we collect it, and how we handle it. We will never sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up we collect:

  • Your email address (used for magic-link sign-in).
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a verified email address and, where permitted by your Apple or Google settings, your name.
  • A display name you choose during onboarding.
  • An optional profile avatar you upload.

Rota and scheduling data. When you create or join a rota we collect:

  • Rota names, descriptions, timezones, and recurrence rules.
  • Scheduled occurrence dates and times, including any manual overrides.
  • Rota membership: which accounts belong to which rota and in what role (owner, member, or viewer).
  • Swap requests between members.
  • Invite codes you create or redeem to share a rota.

Device and notification data. If you enable push notifications we store a device push token issued by your operating system (iOS or Android). This token is used solely to deliver your own reminders and rota notifications.

Diagnostic and error data. We use Sentry to capture anonymised crash reports and performance diagnostics. Sentry may record the app version, device model, operating system version, and a stack trace. We do not intentionally send personal content (e.g. rota names or messages) to Sentry.

Information we do not collect. We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone, camera, or any data unrelated to scheduling. We do not display advertising and do not build advertising profiles.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide the service. To create and manage your account, display your rotas, schedule occurrences, send you push reminders, and process swap requests.
  • Share with your rota members. Your display name and avatar are visible to other members of rotas you belong to. Your email address is never shared with other users.
  • Improve reliability. Crash reports help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Authenticate you. We verify sign-in via magic link (email), Apple, or Google.

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. How we share your information

We share data only as necessary to operate the service:

  • Supabase. Our backend database and authentication infrastructure. Your account and rota data is stored in Supabase's hosted Postgres database. Supabase acts as a data processor under our instructions. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Sentry. Crash and performance diagnostics. See sentry.io/privacy.
  • Apple and Google. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, your email address is verified and passed to us by Apple or Google respectively. See their privacy policies for details on how they handle your sign-in data.
  • Expo / EAS. We use Expo Application Services to deliver push notifications on your behalf. Push tokens are routed through Expo's notification infrastructure. See expo.dev/privacy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (via Settings › Account in the app), we permanently delete your profile, rota memberships, and push tokens within 30 days. Aggregated or anonymised diagnostic data retained by Sentry follows Sentry's own retention schedule.

Rota data you created (rota records, occurrences) may remain visible to other members of that rota after you leave or delete your account, attributed to a deleted user.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over TLS. We use row-level security on our database so that each user can access only the rotas and data they are a member of. Push tokens are stored server-side and never exposed to other users. Authentication tokens (magic links, OAuth) are short-lived and single-use.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please contact us at privacy@gorotini.com before disclosing it publicly.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access. Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction. Update your display name or email in the app at any time.
  • Deletion. Delete your account in Settings › Account. You may also email us to request deletion.
  • Portability. Request an export of your data.
  • Objection / restriction. Object to or ask us to restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent. Where processing is based on consent (e.g. push notifications), you can withdraw by disabling notifications in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@gorotini.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information.

EEA / UK residents. We process your data on the legal basis of contract performance (to provide the service you signed up for) and, for crash diagnostics, our legitimate interest in maintaining a reliable application. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Children's privacy

Rotini is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA / UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, email us at privacy@gorotini.com.
